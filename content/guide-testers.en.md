# A guide for testers

## How the pilot works

Afrocipe is exploring a simple question: can someone who has never cooked this version of a dish make it using only the written recipe?

1. Someone writes a recipe down and sends it in. It appears on this site.
2. Testers choose a recipe from the list. You are one of them. Anyone can be a tester; you do not need to be a trained cook.
3. Each tester cooks it at home, using only what is written.
4. Each tester tells us what happened in a short report.
5. We put the reports together to see how many testers made the dish and where the recipe needs to be clearer.

**We are checking whether the recipe works, not judging your cooking.** If the dish fails, that is a useful result. It tells us the recipe needs work.

**Please do not fix the recipe quietly.** If you changed something, guessed, or made a mistake, tell us in the report. Please record every change, even a small one: it helps us understand what happened.

## Before you cook

- **Join first.** If you have not yet, fill in the short sign-up form: [Join as a tester](form:signup). You only do this once.
- **Choose a recipe** on the [Recipes](recipes.html) page. Recipes with the fewest tests come first.
- **Did you write this recipe, or help with it?** Helping includes giving the recipe to the writer, teaching it to them, or correcting it. If so, please do not test it. Choose another one.
- **Read the whole recipe once** before you start, so nothing surprises you halfway.
- **Use only the recipe page.** Do not look up other versions or videos, and do not ask the person who wrote it. If a step is unclear, do what seems most sensible and tell us in the report.
- **Check the equipment.** Each recipe lists what it needs. Use your own kitchen, and tell us if you used something different.

## While you cook

Follow the recipe as it is written, even where you would normally do it differently. Keep a pen nearby and note these as you go:

- [ ] The time you started and the time you finished
- [ ] Anything you swapped, and why (for example, no palm oil in the shop)
- [ ] Any mistake you made (a missed step, a wrong amount)
- [ ] Any step that was unclear, and what you decided to do
- [ ] Whether what the recipe said you would see, smell or feel at each stage actually happened
- [ ] A photo of the finished dish

If you have to stop before the end, that is fine. Note where you stopped and why, and still send the report.

## After you cook

**Send your report from the recipe page.** The **Report my attempt** button, at the bottom of the screen and at the end of the recipe, opens the report and tells us which recipe you cooked. Finished or stopped early: both are useful. Fill it in the same day if you can, while you still remember. It takes about 10 minutes.

| The report asks | What we mean |
| Overall, did you make the dish the recipe describes? | Judge against the recipe's description, not your own taste. "I cannot tell" is an honest answer. |
| Did the signs the recipe describes along the way happen? | What the recipe tells you to look, smell or listen for, such as "the oil rises to the top". |
| Did it make about as much as the recipe says? | Did it make roughly as much as it said, for example 4 servings. |
| Did it take about as long as the recipe says? | Compare your start and finish times with the recipe's. |
| Did the finished dish match the description or photo? | Look, taste and texture, compared with what the recipe says. |
| Swaps, mistakes, unclear instructions | Three separate questions, from the notes you kept. Leave one blank if it did not happen. |

If you cook the same recipe again, send a new report each time. Every attempt counts.

## Questions and your details

- **Questions about the pilot:** [contact us](form:contact).
- **Questions about a recipe step:** we cannot explain it to you, because that would spoil the test. Do your best and record it as an unclear instruction.
- **Your details:** your name and email are kept separate from your report. Published results never identify you, and you can withdraw at any time by emailing us.
