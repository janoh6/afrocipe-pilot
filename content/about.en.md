# About Afrocipe

Afrocipe is building a trusted record of African dishes. Each dish is written down clearly, with where it comes from and who passed it on, and is tried out by independent people cooking at home, so that anyone can see whether the written recipe can really be followed.

## Our mission

**To make African culinary knowledge accessible, attributable, reproducible, and useful to the world.**

We document and structure African culinary knowledge, preserve its origins and cultural context, and develop evidence-based systems that help people learn, reproduce, study, and build upon it.

## Our vision

**A world where African culinary knowledge is understood, valued, and used everywhere, without losing its connection to the people and cultures from which it originates.**

We envision African culinary traditions becoming an integral part of global food culture, education, innovation, and commerce, supported by reliable knowledge that can be passed between generations, communities, and continents.

## About this pilot

The pilot is a first, small trial. People share recipes they know well. Testers cook them at home and tell us what happened. We use the reports to learn where written recipes need to be clearer, and how to run recipe testing well.

- **Who can share a recipe:** anyone who knows a dish well enough to write it down. See the [guide for recipe writers](guide-writers.html).
- **Who is a tester:** anyone who cooks at home, follows a recipe exactly as written, and tells us honestly what happened. You cannot test a recipe you wrote or helped with. See the [guide for testers](guide-testers.html).

## What this site is not

!notice

A recipe stays its writer's. Names, emails and the story of where a recipe comes from are not shown on this site.
