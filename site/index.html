Help us test African recipes

Afrocipe is building a trusted record of African dishes: recipes written down clearly, with where they come from, and tried out by people cooking at home.

This pilot asks one question: can someone who has never cooked a dish make it from the written recipe alone?

Who can take part?

Who can share a recipe?

Anyone who knows a dish well enough to write it down. It can come from your family or community, or be your own creation. You do not need to be a professional cook.

Share a recipe Guide for recipe writers

Who is a tester?

Anyone who cooks at home, follows a recipe exactly as written, and tells us honestly what happened. No training is needed. You cannot test a recipe you wrote or helped with.

Join as a tester Test a recipe Guide for testers

How it works

  1. A writer sends in a recipe. It appears on this site.
  2. A tester picks it and cooks it at home, using only what is written.
  3. The tester sends a short report.
  4. We read the reports together to see where the recipe needs to be clearer.
